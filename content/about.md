I'm Alexander Inkoom, a penetration tester and application security engineer based in Ghana. Most of my time goes into hacking web applications and their underlying APIs, code review, and anything else that catches my interest. Outside of work I hunt on bug bounty programs, dig into publicly disclosed vulnerabilities to understand how they really work, and play CTFs. My team took 2nd place at Break The Code CTF in 2023 and won 1st place in 2024. This blog is where I write up what I find, once it's fixed and disclosure is allowed.
